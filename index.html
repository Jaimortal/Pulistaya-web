Lore & Background

The Legend of Pulistaya

From nostalgic Filipino street games to an electrifying multiplayer digital arena.

In a peaceful Filipino neighborhood, children have always enjoyed playing a traditional game called Pulis Dunggab.

The rules are simple: one player becomes the Police, while the others become the Hiders. The Police must find and catch everyone before time runs out.

But in Pulistaya, the game becomes something more exciting. A mysterious competition has turned the neighborhood into a massive playground. Streets, houses, grassy fields, and hidden locations become the battlefield.

One player is chosen as the Police. Equipped with special abilities, the Police must track down the Hiders before they can escape. The Hiders, however, are not defenseless. Each character has unique abilities that help them disappear, run away, distract the Police, or even fight back.

The Police must use strategy, observation, and timing to catch everyone. The Hiders must work together, use their surroundings, and carefully manage their skills to survive.

Only one side can win. Will the Police catch everyone, or will the Hiders outsmart the hunter?

Game Mechanics

Built for Intense Multiplayer Fun

Designed from the ground up for seamless local LAN matches and cross-device gameplay.

LAN & Mobile Hotspot

Host games right from your smartphone or PC. Friends can join seamlessly by typing in the local IP address or connecting via WiFi.

Cross-Platform Support

Seamlessly play together whether you are on Windows PC with mouse & keyboard, or on an Android smartphone with touch controls.

High-Level ENet Sync

Real-time replication of character movement, rotation, sprint animations, skill triggers, and camera angles with minimal network latency.

Mobile Touch & Virtual HUD

Responsive floating virtual joystick, drag-to-look camera gestures, two-finger pinch zoom, and dedicated tactical skill buttons.

Filipino Neighborhood Maps

Authentic Philippine environmental details: sari-sari store colors, concrete bungalow houses, utility posts, and neighborhood compounds.

Custom Key Rebinding

Fully rebindable keyboard mappings, independent touch and mouse look sensitivities, and persistent device settings.

Behind The Scenes

Development & Open Source Credits

Created in an intensive 3-day development sprint combining human design with modern AI-assisted engineering.

Created in a Total of 3 Days — Made For Fun

Please don't expect a AAA commercial release! This game was engineered from scratch in just 3 days as a creative experiment, learning project, and passion tribute to the Filipino street game Pulis Dunggab.

Lead Designer & Developer

Jaime Perez

Designed the game concept, mechanics, and orchestrated the entire system architecture. Designed the multi-agent AI workflow that generated and assembled the code, assets, maps, and logic into a playable Godot 4 multiplayer title.

100% Free & Open Source

No Store Release • Pure Passion

Pulistaya has no intention to be released on Google Play Store or any commercial marketplace. The entire codebase is open-source on GitHub so other developers and Filipino students can study, modify, and build upon it freely.

Platform: Android APK only • License: Open Source (MIT)

AI & Software Toolchain
